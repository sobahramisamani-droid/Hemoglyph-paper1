## Reading of the results (honest)
* **MIMIC-IV demo.** The criterion was written with the eICU-style primary-diagnosis metric in mind; the MIMIC script scores *every* coded diagnosis of the admission (multi-label, ICD-9/10), which is a harder reference — but the criterion is the criterion and it is failed. The 14.1 vs control difference (−2.2 points at top-5) is almost entirely CKD (27.7 vs 50.8 % recall@5): 14.1 reports a single-panel reduced GFR as "chronicity undetermined" (KDIGO) and reserves CKD for documented chronicity, so ICD-coded CKD is no longer "recalled" from one panel. Groups that the 14.x work targeted improved: AKI 56.7 vs 52.2, renal impairment (union) 65.6 vs 61.3, acute myocardial infarction 71.4 vs 42.9 (n = 7). Chart support of confident outputs: 57.1 % (lab-detectable) vs 62.9 %.
* **Sepsis set B.** Registry 14.1 adds nothing measurable over 13.3.0 under the same engine (0.638 vs 0.639); the SIRS/qSOFA syndrome layer reduces the control flag rate (16.1 vs 19.0 %) at the cost of recall@5 (33.2 vs 37.2 %). The improvement over the published 13.3.0 number (set A, old engine, 0.534) is therefore attributable to the engine (unit-alias fix, ESL screening) and to the cohort, not to the sepsis rules. The supervised logistic reference on the same features is 0.744–0.751; a laboratory-only rule engine without trajectories or clinical text does not approach it. Earlier expectation (0.65–0.72) was wrong and is recorded as such.
* **NHANES H.** Population-level discrimination is reproduced on a different cycle; the one material registry effect is ALT elevation (+0.14 over control), from the MASLD/ALT rules added in 14.1.
* **Safety.** No healthy false assertion in 1,000 synthetic healthy adults; NHANES guideline-healthy false-assertion 1/246 (same in both arms).

## What this means for the paper
Registry 14.1 / engine v6 is a **correctness release** (ABG units, KDIGO chronicity, UDMI injury-vs-infarction, explained-finding ordering, SIRS/qSOFA, MASLD, PSGN) whose benefit is visible on the specific targets (AKI, MI, ALT, flag rate) and on probe behaviour, **not** a global accuracy gain; on coded-diagnosis recall it is neutral to slightly negative because codes reward one-panel CKD labelling that KDIGO forbids. The paper reports 13.3.0 (pre-registered) as primary and 14.1 as the current release with these validation numbers, including both failed criteria.
