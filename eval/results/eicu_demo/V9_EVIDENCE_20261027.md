# v9 — evidence before any change (2026-10-27; frozen v7 outputs, eICU-CRD demo)

Rule of the project: evidence first, change second. This note records why no v9 rule change is made.

## 1. Where the engine trails a supervised model on identical inputs (B2, per-group AUROC)
DKA 0.86 vs 0.96; GI bleeding 0.75 vs 0.85; diabetes 0.69 vs 0.75; AKI 0.80 vs 0.83. Sepsis / alcohol withdrawal / heart failure: outcome-defined, no laboratory definition — out of scope for rules.

## 2. DKA — the 40 top-5 misses among 108 coded stays, classified by what the first panel contains
| class | n | guideline position (ADA/EASD 2024 hyperglycaemic crises) | rule change possible? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| glucose ≥ 250 but bicarbonate ≥ 18 | 20 | DKA criteria not met in this panel (resolving/treated DKA; label is admission-level) | no — asserting DKA here would violate the criteria |
| glucose < 250 | 12 | criteria not met (treated or euglycaemic) | no |
| glucose only, no bicarbonate/AG/pH (glucose 382–896) | 5 | cannot diagnose DKA/HHS without acid–base; "hyperglycaemic crisis — obtain bicarbonate, ketones, osmolality" is a next-test prompt, not a diagnosis | no (next-test already issued); label-map change would be post hoc |
| glucose ≥ 250 and bicarbonate < 18 — genuine engine miss | 2 | criteria met | yes, but n = 2 (0.08 % of scorable labels) |
| no glucose | 1 | — | no |

Supervised AUROC 0.96 is reached by learning that very high glucose in this ICU population *is* DKA — a prevalence/prior effect, not a criterion.

## 3. GI bleeding — the 36 misses among 149 coded stays
| class | n |
|---|---:|
| Hb ≥ 10 and BUN:Cr < 30 | 15 |
| Hb < 10 and BUN:Cr < 30 | 12 |
| BUN:Cr ≥ 30 (any Hb) | 6 |
| no Hb | 3 |
BUN:Cr ≥ 30 occurs in 49/145 coded GI-bleed stays vs 297/2,264 overall (LR+ ≈ 2.6): a supportive feature at best; adding it recovers ≤ 6 labels (0.25 points).

## 4. Hybrid arm (eval/hybrid_arm.py; group-level recall@5, same 2,432 labels)
engine 58.8 → hybrid max(engine, ML) 60.6 (+1.8, 95 % CI +1.2 to +2.5); hybrid restricted to the six outcome groups 59.8 (+1.0). ML alone 68.8 (+10.0) — obtained through sepsis 94 %, diabetes 91 %, CKD 65 % (prior learned from this cohort), while losing most single-analyte groups (hyponatraemia 38 %, hypomagnesaemia 3 %, metabolic acidosis 12 %).

## 5. Decision
Guideline-sourced rule additions available: ≈ +0.3 points total. Hybrid: ≈ +2 points, at the cost of the "untrained" claim. Neither justifies a v9 release before submission. Earlier arms (full in-window time series: 63.7 → 63.2; documented history: +2.9, mostly echo) point the same way. The number is bounded by the reference standard; the route to a higher *true* figure is a panel-level adjudicated reference (Paper 2), not engine changes.
