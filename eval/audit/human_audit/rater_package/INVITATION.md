# Invitation — independent source-concordance audit

**Subject:** Invitation to act as an independent rater in a 100-rule concordance audit (≈8–15 hours)

Dear Dr …,

I am Sobhan Bahrami, a student at Allameh Helli 10 High School (NODET), Tehran. Over the past two years I have built **Hemoglyph**, an open-source, offline engine that ranks a source-cited differential from laboratory panels. Its rule base (≈33,000 executable rules) was largely drafted with large language models and then verified with four reproducible methods; the methods paper is prepared for *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine*.

The paper's declared weakness is that the question "does the cited source actually state this threshold?" has so far been answered by a single AI rater. A protocol was therefore frozen *before any rater was approached* (random sample of 100 rules, C/D/U verdict criteria, pre-specified outcomes), and the AI verdicts were sealed so that human raters rate blind.

**What I am asking.** For each of 100 rows, consult the cited source at the given URL/DOI and record whether it states the same analyte/operator/threshold/unit/population (C), something different (D), or cannot be determined from that source at that locator (U), with a verbatim quote for every C and D. The question is **not** whether the rule is clinically sound—only whether it matches its source. At most 15 minutes per item.

**Conditions.** Independence from the project, no financial relationship, no prior sight of the AI verdicts. Two independent raters are required (laboratory medicine/clinical pathology or internal medicine). Raters are named in the Acknowledgements and, with substantive contribution to interpretation, invited as authors of the audit report. Raw verdicts of both raters will be published whatever the result.

Attached: rater form (xlsx), instructions, protocol (Supplementary Note 19).
